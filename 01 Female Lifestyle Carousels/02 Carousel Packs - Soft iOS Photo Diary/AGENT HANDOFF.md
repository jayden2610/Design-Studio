## Direction 2 typography recipe to recreate as native Canva text

Use this for the eventual five-slide editable build:

1. **Main headline:** DM Sans ExtraBold or Bold, lowercase, 96–120 px, tight line height. Use 2–3 short words, e.g. `weekend` / `notes`.
2. **Offset/shadow:** duplicate the headline in Warm Blush `#F4D8D5`, offset roughly 6–10 px down/right beneath the Cream `#FFF9F2` main layer. Keep both text layers editable.
3. **Romantic accent:** Cormorant Garamond Italic, 36–52 px, one phrase only, e.g. `in my little life`.
4. **Metadata:** DM Sans Medium, 15–18 px, uppercase, lightly tracked; example: `SLOW WEEKEND · AUGUST`.
5. Place type only in the photo's natural negative space. Avoid covering the creator's head, hands, mug, notebook, or the main photo story.
