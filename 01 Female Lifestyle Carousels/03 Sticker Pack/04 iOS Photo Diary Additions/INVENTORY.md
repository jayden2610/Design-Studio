## Pack logic

- The original 30 PNGs supply the tangible feminine lifestyle world: coffee, skincare, books, fashion, desk objects, movement and home.
- This extension supplies the composition language: generic soft mobile UI, photo-diary frames and hand-drawn annotations.
- No Apple logos, app logos or copied proprietary interface screens are included. The UI is generic and iOS-inspired only.
