# Seller actions before the final Canva Business step

These are the only remaining actions that require the seller rather than the prepared product package. Complete them before publishing an Etsy listing.

## 1. Confirm rights and accuracy

- [ ] Confirm you created the carousel layouts and the listing images yourself.
- [ ] Confirm every photo, sticker, font, and graphic in every buyer-facing Canva design is either your own, generated by you under terms that permit commercial use, or licensed for resale inside an editable Canva template.
- [ ] If a Canva design contains Canva Pro, Branded, Education, or third-party stock content, check the item's specific licence before selling an editable template. Replace anything that cannot be passed through in a buyer-editable design.
- [ ] Confirm the listing images accurately show what a buyer receives: five separate four-page carousels, not access to a shared Canva folder.
- [ ] Confirm whether the buyer receives the existing placeholder photos. If not, change the sentence in `ETSY-LISTING-COPY.md` before publishing.

## 2. Set up the Etsy shop

- [ ] Open or verify the Etsy seller account using your own legal identity.
- [ ] Complete payment, bank payout, tax, and identity verification in Etsy.
- [ ] Set a shop name, icon, banner, About section, shop announcement, and support email/message workflow.
- [ ] Add the digital-product policies that match your location and customer-service policy. Do not rely on a generic "no refunds" statement where consumer law requires otherwise. 

## 3. Make commercial choices

- [ ] Choose the listing price and currency.
- [ ] Decide whether to launch with an introductory sale and its end date.
- [ ] Set automatic or manual renewal.
- [ ] Decide whether the bundle is personal-use only, business-use for the buyer's own brand, or includes commercial client work. Keep that choice consistent in the listing and PDF licence.

## 4. Finalise only after Canva Business is enabled

- [ ] Generate five actual Canva **Template links** from the five `EDIT THIS FIRST` buyer copies.
- [ ] Test each link using a separate Canva account or incognito browser. Confirm it offers **Use template** and produces a fresh copy.
- [ ] Add those links to `template-links.json`.
- [ ] Run `create_access_pdf.py`, inspect the PDF, and upload it to Etsy.
- [ ] Create the Etsy listing draft, add the eight gallery images and listing copy, then review and publish.

## Never use as a substitute

- Normal Canva edit links
- Shared Canva folders
- Public-view links
- Canva masters, PPTX files, source HTML, or source-asset folders

These options either expose the seller workspace or do not give the buyer a safe editable copy.
