# Soft Story Studio - mock Etsy brand identity

Working brand for the Female Lifestyle Carousel Template bundle. Use it as a starting point; change the name if Etsy says it is unavailable. Do not use `Studio` in the public shop name if you want a more casual creator-led tone.

## Brand position

**Soft Story Studio** creates warm, editable Canva templates for creators who want their everyday moments to feel like a thoughtful story - not a hard sell.

**Audience:** lifestyle creators, bookish creators, cosy-business owners, social-media managers, and personal brands that want a feminine, calm, human feed.

**Promise:** Make a polished, personal carousel in the time it takes to swap your photos and words.

## Etsy shop details

| Field | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Shop name to try | `SoftStoryStudio` |
| If unavailable | `SoftStoryCo`, `SoftStoryTemplates`, `SoftStorySocial`, `SoftStoryDesigns` |
| Shop title | Editable Canva Templates for Soft, Story-led Social Content |
| Shop announcement | New: 5 editable lifestyle carousel templates for warm, story-led Instagram posts. Download, customise, and post in minutes. |
| About opening | Soft Story Studio makes editable Canva templates for the little moments worth sharing - slower mornings, weekend notes, photo dumps, and everyday stories. |
| Support channel | Etsy Messages |

## Voice

- Warm, clear, slightly editorial.
- Say: **make it yours**, **your everyday stories**, **edit and post**, **soft, story-led content**.
- Avoid: hustle language, "go viral" promises, vague luxury claims, or saying templates guarantee reach, sales, or engagement.

## Visual identity

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Primary | Cocoa ink `#342E3F` |
| Paper | Warm paper `#FBF3E6` |
| Accent | Dusty berry `#7A3C57` |
| Secondary | Muted olive `#9CA274` |
| Display type | DM Sans Bold / ExtraBold |
| Supporting type | Poppins Medium / SemiBold |
| Image mood | Warm light, everyday interiors, journals, coffee, books, quiet detail shots |

## Shop icon concept

Use a warm-paper square with the letters **SS** in cocoa ink, set in DM Sans ExtraBold. Add one small dusty-berry star or hand-drawn underline at the lower right. Keep it simple enough to read at Etsy's small icon size.

## Banner copy

```text
SOFT STORY STUDIO
Editable Canva templates for the little moments worth sharing.
```

## AI disclosure for this bundle

Put this sentence near the end of the Etsy description:

> Designed with the assistance of AI and refined into editable Canva templates. All layouts and listing materials are original to Soft Story Studio.

This is transparent and matches Etsy's requirement to disclose seller-prompted AI creations. Do not make an inaccurate claim that the designs are entirely human-created.

## First-shop setup order

1. Try `SoftStoryStudio` as the Etsy shop name.
2. Use the shop title, announcement, and About opening above.
3. Create a simple `SS` icon and the banner copy in Canva.
4. Keep shop sections minimal at launch: `Carousel Templates` and `New Releases`.
5. Create the bundle listing after the safe Canva Template links are finished and tested.
