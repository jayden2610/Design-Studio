# Etsy customer-support responses

## Can't find the download

Hi! Your purchase is delivered through Etsy. Please sign in through a web browser (not the Etsy app), then go to **Your account > Purchases and reviews > Download files**. The PDF contains all five Canva access links. If you still cannot find it, send me your Etsy order number and I will help.

## Canva link doesn't open

Hi! Please try opening the link from the downloaded PDF in Chrome or Safari while signed in to Canva. If it still does not open, send me a screenshot of the message you see and your Etsy order number. I will check the link for you.

## Buyer edited the wrong thing

Hi! No problem - reopen the original Canva link from your Etsy PDF and select **Use template** again. Canva will create a fresh copy, so you can start again without affecting the original template.

## Buyer wants more templates

Hi! Thank you so much. This bundle includes five four-page carousel designs. I am working on additional styles, so please favourite the shop to see future releases.

## Refund request

Hi! I am sorry the product did not meet your expectations. Because this is a digital product, access is delivered immediately after purchase. Please send me the issue you are having and your order number first - I am happy to help troubleshoot access or template use. Your statutory consumer rights are not affected.

## Resale/right-to-use question

Hi! Your purchase includes a licence for your own personal or business social-media content. You may customise and publish the finished posts, but you may not resell, share, redistribute, or sublicense the Canva templates themselves.
