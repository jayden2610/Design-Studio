## Edit the carousel

### Replace a photo

1. Click the photo you want to change.
2. Choose **Replace**.
3. Upload your own image, then drag it to adjust the crop.

### Change text

1. Double-click any text.
2. Type your own words.
3. Keep a similar amount of text for the cleanest layout; shorten copy if it starts to feel crowded.

### Change colours or details

Click a background, card, shape, or label to adjust it from Canva’s colour controls. You can also move or delete decorative elements to suit your brand.

## Download for Instagram

1. In Canva, select **Share → Download**.
2. Choose **PNG** for the sharpest quality.
3. Select **All pages**.
4. Download and post the images in page order as an Instagram carousel.
