# Agent Handoff — Faceless Lifestyle Reel Video Library

## Objective

Build a commercially usable, faceless b-roll library for **The Soft Sunday Edit**: a calm female-lifestyle account about small routines, camera-roll storytelling, and posting without showing a face.

The clips will become edited Instagram Reels and may support carousel mock-ups. They are **not** a stock-video product, a resale bundle, or assets to place unedited inside a Canva template for sale.

## Deliverables

Source and log **36 approved clips**: six per category below. Quality matters more than exact count; reject near-duplicates.

```text
videos/
  02-faceless-lifestyle-library/
    01-morning-rituals/
    02-home-and-reset/
    03-creative-desk/
    04-out-and-about/
    05-little-details/
    06-light-and-seasons/
    SOURCES.md
    REJECTED.md
```

Each final clip should be 5–12 seconds, at least 1080 px on the short edge, stable, unwatermarked, and ideally vertical 9:16. High-resolution landscape clips are acceptable only if they can crop vertically without losing the action.

## Approved sources and licensing check

Use only the original asset pages from the following sources:

1. **Pexels** — commercial social use is allowed under the Pexels license, but do not redistribute clips as standalone assets or imply endorsement by people/brands shown: <https://www.pexels.com/legal-pages/license/>.
2. **Pixabay** — commercial use is allowed subject to its license; reject clips with recognizable logos, brands, or other rights concerns, and never distribute them standalone: <https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/>.
3. **Mixkit** — use clips only when the specific clip is marked **Video Free License**. Do not use Video Restricted License clips because they are personal/non-commercial only: <https://mixkit.co/license/>.

Do not use Google Images, Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, marketplace listings, repackaged “b-roll bundles,” watermarked previews, or unverified AI-generated footage.

## Art direction

The library must feel like one believable camera roll:

- warm daylight, cream and wood interiors, muted olive/blue, soft shadows, gently lived-in—not luxury hotel or influencer-ad glossy;
- a woman implied by hands, back, shoulders, legs, a cropped outfit, or shadow only; **no recognizable face at any point**;
- small, useful motions: pouring, placing, writing, opening, choosing, walking, arranging, turning a page, drawing curtains;
- ordinary places: kitchen, bedroom, desk, shelf, local café table, path, park bench, market flowers;
- uncluttered negative space that can take native on-screen text.

Reject clips with visible logos, legible private data, app screens, copyrighted artwork as a focal point, strong beauty/fashion advertising energy, obvious posing, heavy filters, exaggerated slow motion, or a face entering the frame.

## Selection rules

Every approved clip must pass all of these checks:

- [ ] The original asset page and creator are recorded.
- [ ] The clip’s license is confirmed on the date downloaded.
- [ ] No visible face, recognizable brand, logo, license plate, private screen, or prominent copyrighted work.
- [ ] Natural motion begins early enough to make a strong first second in a Reel.
- [ ] The key action can survive a 9:16 crop with room for text.
- [ ] The clip is visually distinct from the existing selection.
- [ ] The clip matches the warm, low-contrast, lived-in direction.
- [ ] It contains no baked captions, music, transitions, or watermark.
