# Image Sourcing Agent Brief — Soft iOS Photo-Diary Carousel Kit

## Objective

Source a small, cohesive, commercially usable photo library for the **Soft iOS Photo-Diary Carousel Kit**: a Canva Free-friendly product for female lifestyle and personal-brand creators.

The images will demonstrate editable carousel templates and Etsy listing previews. They should feel like a real, softly documented camera roll—not luxury editorial advertising, influencer stock, or a polished brand campaign.

## Non-Negotiable Licensing Rules

1. Source from platforms whose licence permits commercial use in a digital-template context, such as **Pexels** and **Unsplash**. Prefer Pexels where possible.
2. Do **not** source from Google Images, Pinterest, Instagram, Etsy listings, paid-stock previews, screenshots, or images with unclear rights.
3. Do **not** include recognisable trademarks, app interfaces, copyrighted artwork, magazine covers, or legible private information.
4. Do **not** rely on an image merely being labelled “free.” Read its source licence and record the original source URL.
5. Keep a source record for every selected image. Do not download or use anything whose licence or source page cannot be verified.
6. These images are for styled examples and listing previews. Unless separately confirmed as suitable for redistribution, the buyer-facing Canva templates should contain easy-replace photo frames rather than bundled stock photography.

## Deliverables

Create a folder of **36–45 images**, all with a source log. Aim for 4–6 usable images per content use case, with a few flexible detail images reusable across examples.

### Required folder structure

| Filename | Category | Source platform | Original URL | Creator (if shown) | Licence checked | Notes |

Use stable, descriptive filenames. Never use a downloaded platform’s random filename.

## Image Art Direction

### Overall feel

- Natural camera-roll intimacy: everyday, lightly imperfect, warm and personal.
- Soft daylight, window light, flash-digital-camera feel, or gentle indoor light.
- Slightly desaturated, non-HDR, and not overly styled.
- Mostly vertical images or images that can crop well into 4:5 / square / 3:4 frames.
- Leave enough breathing room for crop flexibility; avoid close crops that cut off the main subject.

### Do choose

- café tables, matcha/coffee, books, flowers, simple meals, skincare, desks, journals, bedding, tote bags, travel details, city walks, hotel details, analog camera / film details, headphones, laptop moments.
- people photographed from behind, hands, friend groups at a distance, or a single natural selfie as optional warmth.
- real homes, shared moments, everyday messiness in moderation.

### Do not choose

- product packshots on pure white backgrounds.
- high-fashion portraits, overt luxury scenes, overly staged influencer posing, or corporate office photography.
- busy tourist landmarks, heavy filters, hard neon, or extremely dark images.
- photos in which a brand logo, copyrighted art, or phone/app screen becomes the focal point.
- images that look too obviously generated or duplicated in lighting/style.

### Face policy

The kit should suit both face-visible and faceless creators.

- Target split: **70–80% faceless** (objects, places, hands, partial body, back-of-head) and **20–30% face-visible or people**.
- Include only **2–4** images with a recognisable face or close friend-group. They should look candid, not posed.
- Every use case must work with faceless buyer photos. Faces demonstrate warmth, not a requirement.

## Quality Gate Before Adding an Image

Select an image only when all answers are “yes.”

- [ ] Commercial-use source and original URL are recorded.
- [ ] It feels believable in a female lifestyle creator’s phone gallery.
- [ ] It can crop cleanly into a 4:5, square, or small collage frame.
- [ ] It has no focal logo, private data, recognisable unauthorised artwork, or confusing brand claim.
- [ ] It is visually compatible with cream, blush, powder blue and pale lavender.
- [ ] It adds a story beat that is not already represented by another selected image.
- [ ] It will still look clear at Instagram mobile size.

## Four Reusable Backgrounds — Locked System

Create these as **editable Canva colour backgrounds**, not stock-image backgrounds. A very subtle Free-compatible paper/grain overlay is optional; it must remain low contrast and never prevent buyers from recolouring a page.

| Background name | Hex | Best use | Build spec |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cloud Paper | `#F8F5F2` | Default covers, collages, calm text slides | Flat colour; optional 2–3% warm paper grain |
| Warm Blush Wash | `#F4D8D5` | Sunday Reset, self-care, intimate close slides | Flat colour or extremely subtle cream-to-blush gradient |
| Powder Blue Utility | `#DCEAF2` | iOS cards, monthly favourites, practical list slides | Flat colour; optional 3% cool grain |
| Pale Lavender Diary | `#E6DDED` | reflective moments, endings, little life updates | Flat colour or extremely subtle lavender-to-cream gradient |

### Background constraints

- No photographic backgrounds; the buyer’s photos are the visual texture.
- No strong patterns, checkerboards, coloured paper tears, or large decorative illustrations.
- Keep each as one easy-to-recolour base rectangle in Canva.
- Use Cloud Paper on roughly 50% of pages; use the other three as accents so the kit stays coherent.

## Easy-Replace Canva Photo Frames — Build Specs

Create frame layouts using Canva’s native photo frames/grids wherever possible. A buyer must be able to drag a new photo onto the frame, double-click to reposition/crop, and preserve the page layout.

### Frame components to create

| Frame ID | Name | Layout / shape | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F01 | Hero Portrait | One large 4:5 rounded rectangle | Covers, story slides, close slides |
| F02 | Two-Up Diary | Two vertical 3:4 rounded rectangles with unequal widths | side-by-side moments / before-after |
| F03 | Three-Photo Stack | One large square plus two small 3:4 images | short narrative sequence |
| F04 | Four-Photo Grid | 2 × 2 grid with small consistent gutters | photo dump / monthly recap |
| F05 | Film Strip | Three landscape 4:3 or 3:2 frames in a vertical strip | weekend diary / timeline |
| F06 | Polaroid Pair | Two slightly offset square frames on cream mats | happy moments / scrapbook emphasis |
| F07 | Editorial Detail | One wide landscape 16:9 or 3:2 frame | location, table setting, scenery |
| F08 | Mini Memory Tiles | Five small mixed-ratio frames around a title | closing collage / life lately |

### Frame system rules

- Use a warm cream (`#FFF9F2`) photo mat where a border is needed; do not bake the image into the border.
- Use rounded corners only on functional cards and Hero Portrait / Two-Up frames. Keep film, Polaroid and postcard frames more tactile.
- Keep a 24–32 px internal frame gutter in Canva; it should remain visible after a buyer replaces images.
- Label master-file frames with unobtrusive helper text only when needed, e.g. “Drop photo here.” Remove helper text from styled preview pages if it makes them look unfinished.
- Never clip an image through a manually masked PNG. Native Canva frames are mandatory for easy replacement.

- [ ] 36–45 selected, categorised images are present.
- [ ] `SOURCES.md` includes a verified source URL and licence check for every file.
- [ ] Image mix is 70–80% faceless and 20–30% face-visible/people.

- [ ] Backgrounds use the four locked hex values and are editable base rectangles.

- [ ] No Canva Pro assets, fonts or backgrounds have been used.

## Handoff Back to the Designer

When complete, provide:

1. The image-library folder and `SOURCES.md`.
2. A brief note identifying the strongest 5–7 images for the first mock carousel.
3. The Canva design link or component page containing the four backgrounds and F01–F08 frame system.

The first carousel to build after this handoff is **Weekend Photo Diary**. It is the best proof-of-concept because it demonstrates photo-first storytelling, the iOS layer, diary frames, and both faceless/optional-face usage.
