# Female Lifestyle Carousel — Visual Contract

This is the shared contract for the active system. Layouts may change; these rules stay locked.

## Mood

Quiet, intimate, feminine, lived-in, reflective, and lightly editorial. The work should feel like a polished personal camera roll, not a loud scrapbook or a glossy ecommerce ad.

## Photography

- Use natural or believable window light.
- Keep the colour grade warm, soft, and low-contrast.
- Use everyday rituals, interiors, objects, movement, and occasional portraits.
- Let the photo remain the main subject.
- Keep type out of faces, hands, mugs, notebooks, and other important story areas.

## Palette

- Warm paper: `#FBF3E6`
- Soft blush: `#F4D8D5`
- Cocoa ink: `#342E3F`
- Muted olive: `#9CA274`
- Optional powder blue: `#DCEAF2`
- Optional pale lilac: `#E6DDED`

Use one accent direction per carousel. Do not introduce saturated red, bright blue, or heavy black backgrounds.

## Typography

Use the Reading Nook Editorial type system:

- `Fraunces` for large lowercase editorial headlines and short reflective copy.
- `Manrope` for captions, metadata, labels, counters, and supporting copy.
- Do not use the legacy Baloo 2, Poppins, or Playfair directions in active Reading Nook packs.

Use a clear three-level hierarchy: headline, supporting copy, metadata. Keep the HTML fallback stacks explicit (`Fraunces, Georgia, serif` and `Manrope, Arial, sans-serif`) and do not add handwriting or novelty fonts to the core system.

## Composition

- Use generous margins, approximately 64–80 px at 1080 × 1350.
- Give every slide one dominant visual anchor.
- Use captions when the pattern calls for them; captions are not mandatory on every slide.
- Use stickers and UI elements as restrained finishing details, not as the subject.
- Keep text editable and assets independently selectable.

## Image-aware contrast

Every slide must be judged against the actual image behind the text. Never approve typography from a flat colour swatch alone.

- Check every headline, caption, label, and metadata line at the final crop and at mobile-preview size.
- If the image is busy, bright, or close in value to the text, add a soft gradient, translucent paper wash, photo mat, or small caption card.
- Use cocoa ink on pale or washed areas and warm paper / white text only on genuinely dark areas.
- Keep faces, hands, mugs, notebooks, and other story anchors clear; move the text or adjust the image crop before adding a heavy overlay.
- Contrast support must remain editable and should never flatten text into the photograph.
- If readability still fails after one subtle support layer, change the layout rather than stacking more effects.

## Never allowed

- Opaque text boxes hiding the person or main image.
- Dense scrapbook layouts with equal visual weight everywhere.
- More than 1–2 decorative stickers per slide.
- Mixed font personalities or random palette changes.
- Text flattened into the image background.
- Negative-space typography as an active direction for this version of the system.
