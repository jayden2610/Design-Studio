# Playful Scrapbook Social — Layout Patterns

These are the approved variables for the Playful Scrapbook Social system. They all obey `PLAYFUL-SCRAPBOOK-SOCIAL-CONTRACT.md`.

## 1. Stickered Hero Cover

One dominant photo or photo pair, a large friendly headline, a small date/location label, and 2–3 scrapbook details such as tape, stars, or a flower sticker.

Best for: `in my happy girl era`, `a week in my life`, `little life lately`, and carousel covers.

Rule: the photo or headline must remain the clear hero.

## 2. Paper Note Over Photo

One lifestyle photo supports a paper note, clipboard, or rounded card containing a list, thought, reminder, or mini lesson.

Best for: `things I wish I knew`, `work notes`, `habits that changed my life`, and reflective posts.

Rule: use one main note card. It should feel like an object placed on the image, not a full-screen interface.

## 3. Labelled Photo Grid

Three to five photos form a clean grid or contact sheet. Each image receives one short label plus one shared title strip.

Best for: `places`, `outfit`, `shopping`, `food`, `current obsessions`, and monthly favourites.

Rule: labels identify the photos; they should not compete with a second large headline.

## 4. Camera-Dump Collage

Combine one portrait or hero image with two to four smaller snapshots, Polaroid frames, a camera graphic, tape, or a small caption card.

Best for: `camera dump`, `moments I loved`, `the foods that made me happy`, and weekend recaps.

Rule: establish one large image first, then add supporting evidence. Avoid equal-weight checkerboards.

## 5. Planner / Bonus Card

A quiet cream or blush page presents a checklist, planner preview, prompt, or save/share CTA. Add one small photo, object sticker, or decorative frame so the slide still belongs to the scrapbook world.

Best for: social planners, small-business tips, checklist posts, and carousel closings.

Rule: use this as an occasional close or utility slide, not every second page.

## Suggested five-slide sequence

1. Stickered Hero Cover
2. Paper Note Over Photo
3. Labelled Photo Grid
4. Camera-Dump Collage
5. Planner / Bonus Card or a quieter Stickered Hero Close

## Pattern selection rule

Change the arrangement, not the contract. Keep the cream paper base, two-accent palette, rounded tactile details, short copy, and controlled sticker language throughout one carousel.
