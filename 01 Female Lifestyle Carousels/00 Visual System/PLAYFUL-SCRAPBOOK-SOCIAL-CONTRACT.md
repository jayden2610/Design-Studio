# Playful Scrapbook Social — Visual Contract

This is the second active visual system. It is more expressive than Reading Nook Editorial, but it still follows repeatable rules.

## Position

Playful Scrapbook Social turns ordinary lifestyle photography into a charming, saveable social post. It should feel like a curated desk, a personal moodboard, or a camera-roll scrapbook—not a random sticker sheet or a polished ecommerce advertisement.

## Mood

Cheerful, feminine, nostalgic, tactile, and internet-native. The energy is friendly and slightly maximal, with enough order that the viewer knows where to look first.

## Photography

- Use candid lifestyle moments, food, outfits, beauty, interiors, pets, travel, and small objects.
- Allow flash, digital-camera softness, imperfect crops, and visible everyday context.
- Mix one human or hero image with supporting detail shots.
- Keep a consistent treatment within each carousel: warm highlights, gentle contrast, and slightly softened saturation.
- Do not cover faces, hands, or the main story object with large decorative elements.

## Palette

- Paper cream: `#FFF8F0`
- Blush pink: `#F3C9D8`
- Butter yellow: `#F7E6A7`
- Soft lilac: `#DCCBE8`
- Leaf green: `#B9D5B2`
- Tomato coral: `#E88978`
- Cocoa ink: `#30252B`

Use cream as the base and select two accent colours per carousel. Coral is a small emphasis colour, not a full background. Avoid neon colours, pure black fields, and uncontrolled rainbow palettes.

For the current AI UGC packs, use a restrained **berry + butter + cocoa** combination: deep berry for large type, butter for small tabs and stars, cocoa for card outlines, and cream as the reading surface. Avoid candy pink headline type over warm neutral photography.

## Typography

- **Display:** `DM Sans` ExtraBold or `Baloo 2` ExtraBold for friendly, legible headlines.
- **Support:** `Poppins` Medium or SemiBold for labels, captions, timestamps, and CTA copy.
- **Editorial accent:** `Playfair Display Italic` for one short emotional phrase only.

Keep the headline family consistent within a carousel. Do not mix handwriting fonts, novelty display fonts, and serif headlines on the same slide.

### Type colour on photography

- When a Playful Scrapbook Social headline sits directly on a light or mid-tone photograph, use a bright rose such as `#EC6F96`; use `#DF557A` for a small label or eyebrow.
- Add a subtle cream offset shadow or thin light backing only when it is needed to preserve readability. Bright colour is not a substitute for contrast.
- If the upper-left title cluster still gets lost in the image, move it to a clearer pocket or put the supporting copy on a paper card.

## Scrapbook language

- Use rounded photo corners, paper cards, tape, tabs, hand-drawn circles, stars, flowers, hearts, camera icons, and small doodles.
- Decorative elements should look intentionally placed, not scattered to fill empty space.
- Use 2–5 decorative elements per slide, with one clearly dominant element.
- Keep stickers independently selectable and editable.
- Build stickers as a small cluster around the primary composition: two or three supporting marks are better than isolated decorations in every corner.
- Use a visible paper edge, mat, or card boundary when a scrapbook layer is introduced.

## Composition

- Artboard: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5).
- Safe margin: 56–72 px, with occasional intentional bleed for the hero image.
- Every slide needs one primary anchor: a hero photo, a large title, a featured card, or a dominant collage.
- Mix scale: one large element, two or three medium elements, and small finishing details.
- Use overlap and rotation sparingly: normally 0–6° rotation, never enough to compromise reading.
- Keep copy short: 3–8 words for a headline, 8–24 words for support copy.

## Image-aware contrast

The photograph is part of the layout, so contrast is checked against the actual image—not against the background colour in isolation.

- Preview every text element on its final crop and at mobile size.
- Use cocoa ink on pale image areas and cream / white text only on dark areas with enough tonal separation.
- When the image is busy or the text crosses changing tones, add one soft translucent wash, paper strip, sticker card, or local gradient behind the text.
- Keep the support layer decorative and light enough that the image remains visible; never hide a face, hand, or key object.
- Do not rely on shadows, outlines, or decorative stickers alone to make type readable.
- If a single support layer cannot create clear reading order, move the text or change the crop.

## Never allowed

- Random sticker accumulation with no hierarchy.
- More than two display font personalities on one carousel.
- Opaque decorations covering the subject or making text impossible to edit.
- Every photo receiving the same size, crop, and visual weight.
- Bright blue social-media UI pills as the default accent.
- Flattened text inside the image background.
- Turning every slide into a promo or CTA card.
- Text that becomes unreadable against the photograph at mobile-preview size.

## Relationship to Reading Nook Editorial

The systems may share the same AI UGC photography and outlined lifestyle stickers, but they must not share the same surface rules:

- Reading Nook is quiet, warm, spacious, and photo-first.
- Playful Scrapbook Social is tactile, layered, colourful, and decoration-aware.

Choose one contract at the start of a carousel and keep it consistent through the full set.
