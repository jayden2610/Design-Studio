# Active Layout Patterns

These are the approved variables. They all obey the same visual contract.

## 1. Full-Bleed Editorial Cover

One lifestyle image fills the frame. Use a large headline in natural negative space, with a small caption or metadata line and at most one sticker.

Best for covers, weekend notes, personal reflections, and diary entries.

## 2. Full-Bleed Captioned Story

Keep the photo dominant. Place a headline, short caption, and optional date/location near the bottom. A subtle gradient may support legibility, but never an opaque card over the subject.

Best for rituals, daily-life stories, and personal updates.

## 3. Notes Card Over Photography

Use one or two photos with a small editable Notes-style card containing a list, reflection, or mini timeline. The card supports the photo rather than replacing it.

Best for currently loving, favourites, routines, and recommendations.

## 4. Split-Image Collage

Arrange two or three related images in a clean split or contact-sheet composition. Use one headline and minimal supporting copy. Keep the crop system consistent and avoid making every image equally dominant.

Best for morning routines, objects, places, before/after stories, and lifestyle roundups.

## Suggested four-slide sequence

1. Full-Bleed Editorial Cover
2. Split-Image Collage or Full-Bleed Captioned Story
3. Notes Card Over Photography
4. Full-Bleed Captioned Closing

Use a fifth slide only when the approved source content needs it; do not add one merely to fit an older pattern document.

The archived negative-space experiment is intentionally excluded from this pattern set.
