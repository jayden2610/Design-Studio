# Carousel Design Studio

Premium, editable social templates built around two focused product lines:

1. **Female Lifestyle Carousels** — aesthetic, real-life storytelling templates for female lifestyle creators.
2. **Monochrome Brand Systems** — expressive black-and-white identity directions for personal brands and F&B businesses.

## Working rule

Launch the Female Lifestyle Carousel MVP first. It has the clearest buyer, sales channel, and repeatable production loop. Keep Monochrome as a parallel portfolio and future collection; do not split the first launch across both audiences.

### Canva delivery standard

Every Female Lifestyle Carousel asset should ultimately live in Canva's free plan. Import all finished designs into Canva and ensure they remain fully editable and designable there, so customers can adapt the copy, imagery, colours, and layout without requiring paid Canva features.

## Folder map

- `01 Female Lifestyle Carousels` — active product work, visual assets, design system, and prototypes.
- `02 Monochrome Branding` — F&B and personal-brand explorations, storyboards, and concepts.
- `03 Reference Library` — reusable research, typography systems, and style references.
- `00 Project HQ` — strategy, decisions, and production plan.

## Current focus

Build a cohesive 15-template Female Lifestyle Carousel MVP before expanding to a 30–60-template paid pack.
