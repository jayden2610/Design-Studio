# 🎨 Carousel Design Studio

Design production workspace for two revenue tracks: **Female Lifestyle Carousels** (a commercial Etsy product) and **Monochrome Branding** (portfolio-ready identity kits). Status and priorities are tracked in Linear (canonical), this repo mirrors working files.

## Tracks

### 1. Female Lifestyle Carousel — Etsy product
- **Goal:** 15 editable 4:5 storytelling carousels (covers, Story adaptations, hooks, caption prompts) packaged with delivery PDF, Canva links, guide, licence, mockups, Etsy listing + Gumroad mirror.
- **Project folder:** [`01 Female Lifestyle Carousels/`](01%20Female%20Lifestyle%20Carousels)
- **Linear issue:** [JAY-66 — Female Lifestyle Storytelling — Launch MVP (Roadmap Parent)](https://linear.app/jayden2610/issue/JAY-66)
- **Status:** In Progress (art direction locked; building the AI-UGC asset library now).

### 2. Monochrome Branding — brand/logo kits
- **Goal:** Convert the strongest monochrome explorations into a separate identity collection for personal brands and neighbourhood F&B — logo, icon/illustration language, typography, and rationale per direction; packaging/menu + social mockups; one personal-brand brief and one F&B brief. Republishable to a personal brand later.
- **Project folder:** [`02 Monochrome Branding/`](02%20Monochrome%20Branding)
- **Linear issue:** [JAY-67 — Monochrome Branding — turn three directions into portfolio-ready kits](https://linear.app/jayden2610/issue/JAY-67)
- **Status:** Backlog (starts after the Female Lifestyle MVP is packaged).
