# Publishing and Sales Checklist

Use this after a concept has been refined into a logo system. Treat every product as either a **bespoke identity lead generator** or a **non-exclusive digital asset**, never both without clearly different licensing.

## 1. Finish the commercial-ready kit

- Redraw the selected SVG mark in Illustrator/Figma; convert type to outlines only in final exports.
- Make 6-10 supporting illustrations, 2 packaging mockups, a menu cover, a social-post set, and a one-page mini style guide.
- Export editable source files (AI/SVG), transparent PNGs, print PDF, and a web JPG preview pack.
- Confirm that every illustration, font, and mockup is licensed for resale. Do not use third-party template graphics in an exclusive client identity.
- Package files by `01 Logos`, `02 Illustrations`, `03 Packaging`, `04 Social`, `05 Guidelines`, `06 Licence`.

## 2. Publish it as a portfolio case study

Primary purpose: attract bespoke F&B clients.

1. Publish the complete visual story on [Behance](https://www.behance.net/): brief, logo, four-frame story, packaging, menu, signage, and 3-5 application mockups.
2. Turn the strongest 6-10 frames into an Instagram carousel and short process reel; the first slide should show the final mark at large scale.
3. Add the case study to a simple portfolio landing page with a clear enquiry CTA: `Custom F&B identity systems — enquire`.
4. Submit packaging-heavy work to [The Dieline](https://thedieline.com/) and use [Brand New](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/) to research agencies and attribution—not as a sales storefront.

## 3. Sell the reusable parts

Primary purpose: earn from non-exclusive assets.

| Platform | What to sell there | Important rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Creative Market](https://creativemarket.com/) | F&B illustration toolkit, label templates, editable packaging and menu elements | Sell modular vectors, not the full exclusive brand identity. |
| [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/) | Premade logo packages, café menus, social template bundles | State whether the buyer receives a non-exclusive or one-time exclusive licence. |
| [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) | Bigger kits, process packs, bundle upsells, direct audience launches | Best when your own audience can drive traffic. |
| [Envato/GraphicRiver](https://graphicriver.net/) | Broad-use logo, mascot, and menu templates | Expect higher volume and lower differentiation. |

Recommended first product: a **Monochrome Food Illustration Toolkit** with 40-60 editable food, hand, counter, steam, and packaging motifs; three example layouts; and personal/commercial licences. Keep the First Crumb, Noodle Signal, and Kopi on Foot marks out of that pack if you want to retain them as exclusive portfolio concepts.

## 4. Sell bespoke work

Primary purpose: turn the concepts into commissioned identities.

- Publish an offer on [Contra](https://contra.com/hire/brand-designers-for-food-and-beverage) or [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/services/design/brand-identity-design) for a *custom F&B illustration identity*, not a logo-only gig.
- Sell a fixed scope: strategy call, one hero mark, 6 supporting illustrations, packaging/menu application, social templates, mini guideline, and source files.
- Contract for ownership clearly: client receives exclusive rights only after final payment; you retain portfolio-display rights unless otherwise agreed.
- Quote revisions and delivery milestones separately. Do not promise trademark clearance—recommend a local trademark professional for that work.

## 5. Before launch

- Search the intended brand name in the market and check trade-mark availability before selling it as an exclusive identity.
- Remove all placeholder text, unlicensed fonts, and mockup watermarks.
- Export a black-only version, reversed white-on-black version, small-size mark, and print test.
- Write the licence in plain language: permitted use, prohibited resale, exclusivity, attribution, and number of seats/locations.
