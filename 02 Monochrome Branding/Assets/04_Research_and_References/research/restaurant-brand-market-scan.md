# Restaurant Brand Market Scan

Research window: 8 July - 7 August 2026. Full raw output is in `restaurant-cafe-bakery-illustrated-brand-identity-buyers-and-marketplaces-raw-restaurant-buyers.md`.

## Answer

Sell a **launch-ready food-brand identity**, not a stand-alone logo pack. The likely buyer is an independent cafe, bakery, food truck, quick-service concept, or a restaurant preparing a refresh/opening. They are buying a usable business system: mark, menu, packaging, social templates, signage direction, and a short set of illustrations.

## What the current signal says

- Restaurant-owner discussion asks for work that functions on bags, apparel, social media, menus, flyers and signs; that makes the deliverable bundle more compelling than a single file.
- Current restaurant-brand posts prominently sell the story, shelf presence, restaurant atmosphere, menu, packaging and website together.
- Discovery is referral-led: owners see a restaurant identity they admire, trace the studio, then make contact. Portfolio case studies and social proof are the acquisition surface.
- The social data is directionally useful, but Reddit rate-limited and TikTok hit the configured provider's credit ceiling. Treat it as a market signal, not a market-size measurement.

## Best buyer wedges for these studies

| Buyer | Trigger | Offer to sell |
|---|---|---|
| First-location bakery / bagel / coffee counter | Opening date, lease, packaging order | "Opening Kit" - logo, 2 food illustrations, bag/sticker/cup artwork, menu starter and social launch templates |
| Food truck / pop-up becoming permanent | Needs to look credible before retail expansion | "From stall to shop" identity - mark, signage layout, takeaway packaging and simple web/social direction |
| Independent restaurant refresh | Food is good, visual identity feels generic | "Refresh Sprint" - distilled identity, hero illustration, menu and delivery-packaging rework |
| Hospitality group / operator with several concepts | Needs fast concept testing with a coherent system | Concept-board licence, then a bespoke rollout per location |

## Distribution decisions (locked)

- **Sell now:** Etsy and Creative Market.
- **Primary discovery:** Instagram, Dribbble and Pinterest.
- **Product rule:** list reusable assets only - food-illustration packs, menu templates, packaging templates, pattern systems and presentation mockups. Keep the three portfolio identities as proof of bespoke capability, not stock logos for resale.
- **Conversion path:** a social post leads to a portfolio board; the portfolio board leads to an Etsy or Creative Market product, or a bespoke enquiry for an opening identity.

The execution detail and post plan are in `distribution-plan.md`.

## Where to sell

1. **Etsy** - use for customer-facing, instantly useful food-business assets: editable menu templates, launch bundles, social templates and packaging labels.
2. **Creative Market** - use for designer-facing, reusable source assets: food illustration packs, repeat patterns, menu and packaging systems, mockups and presentation templates.
3. **Your portfolio** - use the three concepts below as bespoke-work proof. Do not list their core identities as downloadable logo templates.

## Positioning recommendation

Call the offer something like **"Illustrated opening identities for neighbourhood food businesses."**

The proof should look like the swipe references: one memorable food ritual or brand character, then visible application on packaging, menu, counter signage, uniforms and social. The logo is the hook; the operating kit is what makes it worth buying.
