# Monochrome Branding Distribution Plan

## Channels and roles

| Channel | Job | What to publish | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Etsy | Convert small business buyers | Editable menu, launch, label and social-template bundles | Product listing |
| Creative Market | Convert designers and agencies | Food-illustration packs, pattern sets, packaging/menu mockups and source templates | Product listing |
| Instagram | Build taste and turn attention into product clicks or enquiries | Reels, process carousels, packaging mockups and Stories | Bio link / DM keyword |
| Dribbble | Establish craft credibility | Polished hero shots and application-heavy project sequences | Full portfolio case study |
| Pinterest | Create long-tail visual discovery | Vertical pins for every hero, packaging mockup, menu and process board | Etsy / Creative Market listing |

## What to sell first

Do not sell the core Gimbap Roll, Noodle Signal or First Crumb identities as ready-made logos. They are the proof that makes a buyer trust the products.

Start with two product families:

1. **Food-business launch templates** - editable menu, opening announcement, loyalty card, story highlight covers, takeaway label and packaging-sticker layouts. Sell on Etsy.
2. **Illustrated food-brand asset packs** - monochrome food illustrations, repeat patterns, framed logo-study layouts, packaging mockups and presentation boards. Sell on Creative Market.

Every listing needs seven images: the hero cover, contents, editable formats, three applied mockups, use cases and a final CTA. Never lead with a flat logo alone.

## Instagram direction

Audience: independent food-business founders, creative hospitality operators and designers who may buy reusable assets. Goal: saves and shares first, then product clicks and bespoke enquiries.

Use three recurring content pillars:

| Pillar | Format | Point of view |
|---|---|---|
| Food ritual | 8-15 second Reel | A familiar food moment becomes a memorable mark |
| System reveal | 6-8 slide carousel | A logo only earns its place when it works on an operating touchpoint |
| Build in public | Stories + short Reel | Show the reduction decisions that turn an overdrawn idea into a useful identity |

### Hook bank

Use the first frame as the hook. Keep it large, visual and specific.

- "A bagel logo should make you want to eat it."
- "I turned one lifted noodle into a restaurant identity."
- "Most restaurant logos die after the logo. This one starts there."
- "This cookie mark began with one break, not a moodboard."
- "What would this logo look like on the thing customers actually hold?"
- "A food logo does not need another mascot. It needs one good ritual."
- "From ink sketch to takeaway packaging in 12 seconds."
- "Would you remember this on a grease-stained paper bag?"
- "The mistake: drawing the entire restaurant. The fix: one moment."

### Weekly cadence

- **Monday:** one Reel - food ritual / transformation.
- **Wednesday:** one carousel - five to eight slides showing the identity system on packaging, menu and signage.
- **Friday:** one static mockup or Dribbble-style hero shot, with a direct save/share prompt.
- **Stories, 3-5 frames on build days:** work-in-progress, a binary poll, the chosen route, then the final application.

Caption CTA rotation:

- "Save this if you are building a food brand."
- "Comment `MENU` and I will send the editable template when it is live."
- "Which application makes the identity feel most real: bag, menu or sign?"
- "The asset pack is in the bio."

## Dribbble direction

Publish one polished hero shot per concept, then a second shot centred on application. The cover image must make sense at thumbnail size: one food ritual, one strong lockup, one physical object. Do not publish sketch dumps or five unrelated logo variants.

## Pinterest direction

Create 4:5 or 2:3 vertical pins from every case study:

1. Hero illustration + brand name.
2. Packaging or menu close-up.
3. "How it was built" process pin.
4. Product contents pin for Etsy or Creative Market.

Use searchable titles, for example: `Monochrome bakery logo illustration`, `Editable cafe menu template`, `Food branding packaging mockup`, and `Restaurant launch social media templates`.

## Three portfolio concepts

### Gimbap Roll - “the slice reveal”

**Concept:** one hand steadies a compact seaweed roll while the cut face reveals the colourless internal pattern. It is tactile, satisfying and immediately legible at small size.

**Portfolio board:** primary illustrated mark, small one-colour stamp, takeaway sleeve, menu cover, window vinyl and two launch social tiles.

**Post:** Reel starts on the cut-face close-up: "Can one slice carry an entire gimbap brand?" Carousel explains why the interior pattern, not a mascot, becomes the visual system.

### Noodle Signal - “the lifted ribbon”

**Concept:** the compact takeaway carton anchors the mark; one lifted noodle makes the signal-like gesture. Keep the hand and carton simple enough that the noodle remains the recognisable line.

**Portfolio board:** primary illustration, compact small mark, carton, chopstick sleeve or napkin, menu cover, window decal and two launch social tiles.

**Post:** Reel hook: "I turned one lifted noodle into a restaurant identity." Carousel: one action, one business cue, six applications.

### First Crumb - “the break”

**Concept:** one thick cookie splits into two irregular halves over a folded bakery sleeve. The break is the memorable moment; the sleeve grounds it in a real purchase.

**Portfolio board:** primary illustration, small cookie-half mark, pastry sleeve, sticker, menu card, counter sign and two launch social tiles.

**Post:** Reel hook: "This cookie mark began with one break, not a moodboard." Carousel: hero drawing to sleeve, sticker and counter sign.

## Definition of done for every concept

- One primary illustration and one reduced small mark.
- Three physical applications: packaging, menu and signage.
- Two social-launch tiles and one 8-15 second Reel.
- One Dribbble hero image plus one application shot.
- Four Pinterest pins.
- One reusable, legally clear asset derived from the system for Etsy or Creative Market.
