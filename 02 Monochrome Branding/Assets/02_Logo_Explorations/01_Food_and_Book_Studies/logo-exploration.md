# Logo Exploration: Illustration-Led Vignettes

This is the active logo direction for the bookstore concept **Second Shelf**. The logo is a frozen illustrated moment from the brand's world, not an abstract symbol or a sequential illustration.

## Shared constraints

- Keep the monochrome hand-drawn ink language.
- Use one standalone vignette, not a sequence.
- Use the simple-story rule: one action, one anchor, one supporting cue.
- Keep only the environmental cue needed to make the moment legible.
- Remove unnecessary surrounding frames.
- Add the hand-lettered wordmark beside or beneath the illustration later.
- Avoid generic badges, seals, abstract cup icons, and compressed emblem shapes.

## Direction A: The Next Book

A single hand places an open book on a small two-shelf bookcase; a tiny sprout grows from the top shelf. The act of placing “the next book” carries the brand idea without showing a full shop.

**Personality:** thoughtful, handmade, quietly optimistic.  
**Best use:** primary mark, website header, bags, bookmarks.  
**Why it works:** the hand and open book create an immediate story, while the small shelf makes the bookstore context clear.

## Direction B: The Window Light

A straight-on shop window has one curtain pulled aside by a small hand; inside are only one desk lamp and one book on the sill. The opening curtain becomes the whole narrative.

**Personality:** curious, intimate, welcoming.  
**Best use:** storefront, website, packaging, signage.  
**Why it works:** the architectural frame gives it a distinct silhouette and avoids repeating the human exchange composition.

## Direction C: The Passing Book

Two hands exchange one wrapped book with a plain paper band. A thin neighborhood street line and one small star are the only supporting cues.

**Personality:** human, communal, generous.  
**Best use:** membership cards, social profile, community campaigns.  
**Why it works:** it preserves the emotional exchange while removing the repeated bookshelf/interior setting.

## Recommended system

Use **The Next Book** as the primary logo, **The Window Light** as the architectural variant, and **The Passing Book** as the community/storytelling variant.

The logo family should remain illustration-led even when reduced. A simplified version may be created later, but it should be derived from the chosen vignette rather than replacing it.
