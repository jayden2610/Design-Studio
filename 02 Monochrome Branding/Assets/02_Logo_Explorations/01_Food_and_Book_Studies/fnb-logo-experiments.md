# F&B Branding Experiments: Bagel Shop

This branch focuses on making the business category legible through a simple branded ritual, not through an isolated food illustration.

## Bagel-shop branding logic

A bagel shop can be recognized through a combination of:

- one unmistakable bagel cue;
- one shop or service cue, such as packaging, a counter, an awning, a receipt, or an opening ritual;
- one human action that gives the mark warmth and memorability.

The formula is: **bagel cue + business cue + human gesture**.

## Review set

### 01 — The Bagel Handoff

A hand slides a small branded paper bag across a counter. One bagel peeks out, with a receipt attached. The bag and handoff communicate takeout, service, and a bagel shop without showing a full interior.

**Composition:** close-up horizontal service gesture.  
**Personality:** warm, everyday, neighborhood, generous.  
**Best use:** primary logo, takeaway bags, stickers, social avatar.

### 02 — Open for Bagels

A small awning carries one round bagel-shaped hanging sign. A hand turns it toward opening, with one tiny coffee-steam line as the morning cue.

**Composition:** compact frontal architectural mark.  
**Personality:** bright, local, morning-oriented, welcoming.  
**Best use:** storefront, website header, cups, opening-hours signage.

These directions are intentionally distinct: the first is a service interaction; the second is a storefront-opening ritual.
