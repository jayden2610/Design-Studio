# Kopi Kita — Portfolio Direction

## Core idea

**Kopi Kita** is a present-day Singapore kopi counter for the familiar, in-between part of the day: direct service, strong coffee, and a small moment of care.

**Customer line:** Kopi, toast, no fuss.

**Primary ritual:** A hand tilts a stainless-steel kopi kettle and cloth filter, pouring dark kopi into a floral porcelain cup on a tiled counter.

| Visual role | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Floral handled kopi cup and saucer |
| Action | One continuous diagonal pour |
| Business cue | Checker-tile counter |
| Primary mark | `../01_Identity_Assets/identity-delivery/kopi-kita-primary-illustration.png` |

## Direction

The world is **working kopi equipment + inherited floral porcelain**, not a generic third-wave cafe. It should feel nostalgic, coffee-brown, sun-worn, useful, and immediately local. The contrast is deliberate: browned brushed steel and aged tile against delicate transferware florals.

### Image-style lock

Every generated environment, mockup, and supporting image uses a warm, nostalgic brown-grade: dark kopi, tobacco-brown shadows, sun-yellowed cream, aged stainless steel, and soft analog grain. Keep the green transferware motif restrained and material-specific. Do not introduce bright daylight whites, cool grey interiors, glossy minimalism, or a clean contemporary cafe palette.

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#16130F` | Primary illustration and typography |
| Bone paper | `#E9DCC8` | Sun-yellowed paper field |
| Kopi | `#3B241A` | Dominant coffee-brown shadow and material depth |
| Aged steel | `#766051` | Warm metallic secondary tone |
| Transferware green | `#2F4131` | Small floral-pattern accent only |

The primary mark stays black ink on bone paper. Colour belongs to the wider brand world, never as a substitute for its silhouette.

### Type and graphic language

- Condensed, workmanlike grotesk for menus, labels, and headlines.
- Custom slightly irregular display lettering for `Kopi Kita`; compose it separately from the raster illustration.
- Botanical transferware floral fragments become the secondary pattern.
- Checker-tile grids and order-ticket rules create layout structure.
- Photography uses 10am tropical sunlight, brushed metal, and honest operational wear.

## Five-deliverable portfolio direction

1. **Opening:** A sunlit counter scene with the controlled Kopi Kita lockup applied to glass or tile; copy on the quiet side of the frame.
2. **Identity:** The approved primary illustration, ritual statement, palette, and type direction on bone paper.
3. **Brand world:** Tile counter, porcelain cup/saucer, laminated menu, order ticket, and a small transferware-pattern application set.
4. **Core illustration:** The exact approved primary PNG isolated on its quietest bone-paper field.
5. **Motion:** The same kettle-and-filter pour enters the floral cup over four semantic states. It must use the approved master’s hero, action, and composition—not a new coffee scene.

## Differentiation from the existing portfolios

- **Bagel:** slow bakery opening becomes direct, all-day coffee service.
- **Gimbap Roll:** precise food assembly becomes a vertical liquid-pour gesture.
- **World:** paper bag / awning and seaweed / bamboo give way to stainless steel, porcelain florals, tile, and tropical light.
- **Applications:** cups, saucers, order tickets, and menus replace the existing packaging and sticker emphasis.
