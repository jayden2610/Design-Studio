## 2) What "Reduction / Lockup / Open Tomorrow" Means Here

### Reduction (04)
* The **primary illustration** (full ritual: hero + hand + one cue) is the expressive asset.
* The **small mark** is derived FROM that silhouette for 24px / 20mm survival. It is not a second idea.
* Rule from `design.md` anti-messiness gate: test as solid black silhouette first. If hero+action+cue still read without hatching/labels, you can add selective interior ink back.

**Caps (reviewer rubric):**
* Fail 24px/20mm = cap **69** — not hireable.
* Generic or texture-dependent = cap **79**.
* Brand-impression requires Name-image lock, Food desire, Personality all ≥3/5 to pass 80+.

### Lockup (02 + applied)
* Primary illustration + wordmark share stroke rhythm, weight, and spacing (USPTO 1213.05 one commercial impression).
* You need 2 lockups per brand: **(i) horizontal** for storefront/menu header, **(ii) stacked/badge** for avatar/stamp/receipt.
* Small mark = hero silhouette alone + one interior cue (no hand). Wordmark placed only after silhouette is clean.

### Open Tomorrow (01 + 03)
* 01 = editorial opening: headline + positioning + **real-scale** hero mockup with the actual lockup applied (not generated text).
* 03 = one hero environment + one category-correct application set (see per-brand table). Must make a real customer believe they could order.
* All readable copy/wordmarks placed as controlled vector/PNG overlays on generated environments. Do not trust image-gen to render text.

Shared craft: Ink `#111111` on white or warm paper `#F7F5EF`, outer contour stronger than interior, 30–45% clear space, hatching in short clusters only inside silhouette, leave half the drawing quiet.

## 5) Shared Build Rules

1. **Prompt/production:** Primary illustration = rendered PNG (gpt-image v2) OR hand ink. Never trace primary with hand-coded SVG. SVG only for derived small mark/icon after PNG approved.
2. **Continuity gates:** Deliverable 04 isolate = exact PNG used in 01/03 mockups and 05 motion K0–K3. No second logo in motion.
3. **Type-image unity:** Choose type before final ink pass; make one deliberate glyph tweak per wordmark.
4. **Application realism:** Check viewing distance, quiet space, material scale, perspective, lighting. 01 must read business type without reading copy.
5. **Instagram/Behance grid:** Export 01–05 at 1080×1350 (4:5) + 1080×1080 variants. Covers = 04 isolated on white or 01 shopfront — never beige editorial alone.
