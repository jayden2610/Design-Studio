## Source of truth

`Explorations/` contains the rendered studies and the JSON analyses used to understand their visual logic. The four core food-logo studies are:

1. Bagel Handoff - a service handoff with a paper-bag silhouette.
2. Gimbap Roll - a food-first rolling gesture.
3. Concha Cooling - a single product placed on a cooling rack.
4. Open for Bagels - a hand turning an opening sign below an awning.

`01 Concepts/` is retained as an archive only. Do not use its previous directions as visual references for new work.

## Make the next illustration

Follow [design.md](./design.md). The short version is:

1. Write one food or service moment with one verb.
2. Define one hero object, one action, and one business cue.
3. Render the illustrated moment with confident clear space.
4. Remove detail before adding it; hatching and props cannot carry the idea.
5. Reduce the winning illustration into a small mark, then add the wordmark.

This creates a focused illustrated logo instead of a crowded mini-world.
