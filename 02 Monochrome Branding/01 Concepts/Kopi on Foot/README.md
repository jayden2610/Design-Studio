# Kopi on Foot

The earlier direction was rejected because the stall window, awning, cup, toast, handoff and walking route formed a miniature scene and leaned on generic coffee shorthand. The rebuild reframes the name around a memorable kopi/kaya breakfast ritual.

## Rebuild directions

### Toast dip — chosen top direction

- **Core cue:** a kaya toast corner dips into a dark kopi oval; the contact is the ritual and the visual signature.
- **Intentionally removed:** stall, awning, walking dots, hand, steam, cup handle, and generic café badge language.
- **Small-size behavior:** the toast block and dark coffee oval remain two clear masses, with the dipping diagonal surviving as the recognition cue.
- **Reviewer score:** 89/100 — strong direction. Silhouette 4.5/5; reduction 4.5/5; F&B distinctiveness 4.5/5; figure-ground 4.5/5; hierarchy 4.5/5; lettering integration 4/5; craft 4.5/5; reproduction 4.5/5.

### Breakfast set

- **Core cue:** a folded kaya toast and a coffee saucer share one compact breakfast-set silhouette.
- **Intentionally removed:** stall, service window, handoff, route, steam, and surface texture.
- **Small-size behavior:** the pairing reads reliably as breakfast, though the saucer is a more familiar coffee cue and less ownable than the dip gesture.
- **Reviewer score:** 82/100 — strong direction. Silhouette 4/5; reduction 4/5; F&B distinctiveness 4/5; figure-ground 4/5; hierarchy 4/5; lettering integration 4/5; craft 4.5/5; reproduction 4.5/5.

Both candidates pass the 24 px digital / 20 mm print check and the positive, reversed, symbol-only, and solid-silhouette tests. The selected review mark is `rebuild/kopi-toast-dip.svg`. “Kopi on Foot” remains the working project name; no literal walking cup is needed for the stronger identity.

## Sensory rebuild

`rebuild/kopi-walk-and-carry.svg` now follows the requested story: a person carries a warm kopi into the day. The forward stride is the “on foot” cue; the cup is the food cue; the simplified silhouette avoids a detailed mascot. Reviewer score: **83/100**. The main risk to refine is distinctiveness, so the walking posture and cup proportions must remain ownable at small size.

## Iteration 2 - ritual mark

`rebuild/kopi-stride-v2.svg` is the validated direction. It makes the walk a single confident gesture, keeps the cup prominent, and uses a human silhouette without a mascot face or scene. Reviewer score: **82/100**. Keep the long stride and cup-hand relationship; remove any temptation to add a stall, route, or facial detail.
