# F&B logo rebuild scoring

The local rebuild standard now uses the F&B brand-impression rubric from the monochrome design reviewer. Score every candidate on name-image lock (18), food desire (16), human ritual (14), personality (14), silhouette (12), system/world potential (10), figure-ground/reduction (8), and lettering/craft/reproduction (8).

Do not present a candidate as 80+ unless name-image lock, food desire, and personality each score at least 3/5. The mark must communicate product + ritual + attitude in one image, not only the food category.
