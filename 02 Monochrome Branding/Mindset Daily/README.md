# Mindset Daily — TikTok logo concepts

All files are editable SVGs sized at **1080 × 1080 px**, for TikTok profile use on a white background.

## Directions

1. **Editorial Pulse** — black disc with a custom `MD` monogram. The strongest profile-image candidate because it holds recognizably at small sizes.
2. **Daily Reset** — an open circular arrow makes the daily-practice promise explicit. More active; less ownable than Direction 01.
3. **Focus Frame** — a wordmark held in an open viewfinder. Best for a broader editorial brand, though the text is less clear at tiny sizes.

## TikTok use

- Upload the full 1080 × 1080 SVG export as PNG; do not crop the artwork manually.
- Keep the logo on pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- The primary marks sit within a central 620 px area, designed to survive the profile's circular mask.
- Use Direction 01 as the avatar and its `MINDSET DAILY` signature for headers, video cover cards, and bio-link pages.

The concept sheet is provided for selection only; do not use it as the profile image.
