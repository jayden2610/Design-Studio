ne warm cookie, one clear break, and a folded sleeve underneath. That's the whole brief. Everything else — the shop, the packaging, the sign above the door — lives downstream of this single illustrated moment.

The mark scales from a napkin-sized stamp to a full window sign without ever needing simplification. It was drawn once, at full detail, and trusted to hold up small.

03

In the world

Three touchpoints, one gesture — the break travels from packaging to counter card to signage without losing its hand.

Application — Packaging

The takeaway sleeve

A single fold, uncoated cream stock, one-colour print. The illustration sits dead centre — no border, no clutter — so the line work does the talking.

Application — Menu

The menu cover

A torn-edge card stamped with the wordmark and a short list — same kraft-and-ink discipline as the packaging, so the counter and the sleeve feel like one object.

Application — Signage

The window sign

Same mark, inverted to hold on dark wood — the small illustration sits above the wordmark exactly the way it does on the sleeve.
