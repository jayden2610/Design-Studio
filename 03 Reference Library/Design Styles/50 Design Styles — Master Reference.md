# 50 Design Styles — Master Reference

> Source: [UX Planet — 50 Design Styles Every Designer Should Know](https://uxplanet.org/50-design-styles-every-designer-should-know-for-better-prompting-56c09d55db62)
> Saved: 25/7/26 — for typography, motion design, and AI prompting reference

---

## 1. Neoclassical
Rooted in Greco-Roman ideals — order, symmetry, refined elegance.
- **Elements:** Serif fonts, columns, laurel wreaths, marble, gold accents, symmetry, muted palettes
- **Vibe:** Formal, timeless, noble — luxury brands, museums, heritage

## 2. Baroque
Opulent, emotional, dramatic. 17th-century excess.
- **Elements:** Flourishes, deep shadows, gold leaf, religious motifs, high contrast
- **Vibe:** Regal, ornate — event invitations, premium packaging

## 3. Aurora
Inspired by the northern lights — flowing iridescent gradients.
- **Elements:** Iridescent gradients, blur effects, soft glows, translucent overlays, wave forms
- **Vibe:** Dreamy, futuristic — wellness apps, tech launches

## 4. Ethereal
Delicate, weightless, otherworldly — made of mist and light.
- **Elements:** Light pastels, gauzy overlays, minimal serif fonts, low contrast
- **Vibe:** Calm, mystical — spiritual brands, high fashion

## 5. Filigree
Intricate decorative ornamentation in gold/silver.
- **Elements:** Delicate line patterns, metallic inks, curves, lace textures
- **Vibe:** Luxurious, vintage — heritage branding, luxury packaging

## 6. Acanthus
Classical architectural foliage ornamentation.
- **Elements:** Leaf motifs, flowing curves, symmetrical vines, stone textures
- **Vibe:** Regal, organic — wedding invites, museum branding

## 7. Anthropomorphic
Human traits on non-human forms.
- **Elements:** Expressive eyes, limbs on objects, character icons, rounded shapes
- **Vibe:** Fun, quirky — children's products, gamified UX

## 8. Pixel Art
Retro computer/video game graphics built with visible pixels.
- **Elements:** Grid visuals, low-res icons, limited palettes, 8-bit/16-bit
- **Vibe:** Nostalgic — indie games, retro themes

## 9. Conceptual Sketch
Rough hand-drawn sketches, raw creative feel.
- **Elements:** Pencil/ink lines, crosshatching, greyscale, sketch paper texture
- **Vibe:** Experimental, idea-driven — portfolios, design journals

## 10. Luxury Typography ⭐
Refined letterforms carrying the brand identity alone.
- **Elements:** Serif fonts, high letter spacing, gold foil, monochrome palettes
- **Vibe:** Elegant, timeless — luxury fashion, high-end editorial

## 11. Japandi
Japanese minimalism × Scandinavian functionality.
- **Elements:** Light woods, beige/gray palettes, clean sans-serif, natural materials
- **Vibe:** Calm, intentional — lifestyle brands, wellness apps

## 12. Memphis
1980s bold, loud, rebellious — geometric shapes, high contrast.
- **Elements:** Squiggles, bright primaries, grids, clashing colors, block shapes
- **Vibe:** Youthful, quirky — creative brands, event posters

## 13. Bohemian
Artistic freedom, earthy charm, globally inspired.
- **Elements:** Rich textures, mandalas, jewel tones, ethnic prints, script fonts
- **Vibe:** Free, soulful — handmade products, travel brands

## 14. Shabby Chic
Vintage charm, distressed finishes, feminine elegance.
- **Elements:** Whitewashed textures, faded florals, cursive type, lace, soft pinks
- **Vibe:** Romantic, cozy — weddings, vintage boutiques

## 15. Farmhouse / Cottagecore
Rural life romanticization, simplicity, slow living.
- **Elements:** Florals, gingham, wood textures, antique finishes
- **Vibe:** Cozy, wholesome — lifestyle blogs, rustic brands

## 16. Victorian
Grand, elaborate, deeply ornate — 19th-century British.
- **Elements:** Ornate serif, damask patterns, deep reds/greens, gilded frames
- **Vibe:** Opulent, historical — book covers, vintage fashion

## 17. Art Deco
Sleek, geometric, sophisticated — 1920s glamour.
- **Elements:** Gold accents, sunbursts, symmetry, angular shapes, jewel tones
- **Vibe:** Glamorous, upscale — luxury brands, cocktail events

## 18. Art Nouveau
Nature-inspired, fluid, decorative — floral and botanical.
- **Elements:** Curving lines, floral motifs, hand-lettered type, earthy palettes
- **Vibe:** Romantic, poetic — artistic brands, artisan packaging

## 19. Mystical Western
Cowboy western × occult mysticism.
- **Elements:** Tarot symbols, cacti, western serif fonts, sun/moon motifs
- **Vibe:** Mystical, rugged — crystal shops, indie music

## 20. Kitsch
Ironic "bad taste" — exaggerated pop culture.
- **Elements:** Clashing prints, outdated fonts, shiny plastic, excess
- **Vibe:** Campy, fun — parody, Gen Z fashion

## 21. Y2K
Late 90s/early 2000s tech culture — cyber-futurism.
- **Elements:** Chrome textures, pixel fonts, metallic finishes, iridescent gradients
- **Vibe:** Futuristic, nostalgic — fashion branding, tech revival

## 22. Bauhaus
Functional, geometric, minimal — form follows function.
- **Elements:** Grid systems, sans-serif, red-blue-yellow palette, basic shapes
- **Vibe:** Rational, structured — architecture, clean brand systems

## 23. Brutalism
Raw, bold, purposefully unrefined — rejects polish.
- **Elements:** Monospaced fonts, grayscale, harsh edges, solid blocks
- **Vibe:** Bold, disruptive — artist portfolios, experimental

## 24. Cybercore
High-tech, hacker aesthetics, sci-fi underground.
- **Elements:** Neon lights, glitch effects, code patterns, green/black
- **Vibe:** Futuristic, chaotic — tech events, gaming

## 25. Synthwave
Retro-futuristic 80s sci-fi — Tron × Blade Runner.
- **Elements:** Grid horizons, VHS textures, neon gradients, chrome type
- **Vibe:** Retro-futuristic — music visuals, arcade interfaces

## 26. Vaporwave
Surreal, satirical 80s/90s nostalgia + consumerism critique.
- **Elements:** Pastel pinks/purples, VHS glitch, Greek busts, Japanese text
- **Vibe:** Ironic, dreamy — indie music, visual art

## 27. Pop Art
Mass media, advertising, comic books — bold and colorful.
- **Elements:** Ben-Day dots, bold outlines, primary colors, speech bubbles
- **Vibe:** Loud, fun — pop culture campaigns, retail

## 28. Bento Box UI
Content in neatly organized block-like sections.
- **Elements:** Rounded modular blocks, subtle shadows, icons, micro-animations
- **Vibe:** Organized, clean — dashboards, portfolios, productivity tools

## 29. Graffiti
Rebellious, raw street culture — hand-painted type.
- **Elements:** Spray paint textures, dripping letters, concrete backgrounds, neon
- **Vibe:** Urban, defiant — streetwear, music festivals

## 30. Tenebrism
Dramatic lighting, intense light/dark contrast.
- **Elements:** High contrast chiaroscuro, black backgrounds, spotlighting
- **Vibe:** Intense, moody — gothic art, cinematic posters

## 31. Gothic
Medieval architecture, dark romanticism.
- **Elements:** Blackletter fonts, stained glass, stone motifs, deep purples
- **Vibe:** Dark, dramatic — fantasy covers, music posters

## 32. Pointillism
Small dots of color forming an image.
- **Elements:** Dot clusters, optical mixing, pastel palettes, fade transitions
- **Vibe:** Artistic, textured — fine art branding, editorial

## 33. Mixed Media
Multiple styles blended — photography, illustration, textures.
- **Elements:** Cutouts, overlays, analog + digital collage, paper textures
- **Vibe:** Eclectic, expressive — magazines, experimental branding

## 34. Steampunk
Victorian × industrial machinery × sci-fi fantasy.
- **Elements:** Copper tones, cogwheels, leather, top hats, serif
- **Vibe:** Adventurous, vintage-futuristic — games, books

## 35. Kawaii
Japanese cuteness culture — childlike, cheerful.
- **Elements:** Pastels, rounded icons, baby faces, blushing cheeks, handwritten type
- **Vibe:** Sweet, joyful — stationery, kids' products

## 36. Coquette
Ultra-feminine, flirty, vintage romantic.
- **Elements:** Baby pinks, pearls, ribbon motifs, dainty serif, hearts
- **Vibe:** Delicate, girly — beauty brands, fashion

## 37. Surrealism
Dreamlike juxtapositions, abstract logic, fantasy.
- **Elements:** Collage, disjointed objects, melting forms, metaphors
- **Vibe:** Dreamy, thought-provoking — concept art, experimental

## 38. Utilitarian
Function-first, clean, direct — military-industrial feel.
- **Elements:** Grid layout, monospaced fonts, muted tones, no decoration
- **Vibe:** Practical, minimal — manuals, utility apps

## 39. Mid-Century
1940s-60s — form, function, organic simplicity.
- **Elements:** Clean lines, retro colors, boomerangs, mod patterns, sans-serif
- **Vibe:** Nostalgic, optimistic — furniture, lifestyle blogs

## 40. Scrapbook
Handmade collage — personal, layered, nostalgic.
- **Elements:** Paper textures, washi tape, Polaroid frames, hand lettering
- **Vibe:** Sentimental, warm — journaling apps, memory branding

## 41. Neo Frutiger Aero
Y2K revival — glossy UI, rounded sans-serif, bubble gradients.
- **Elements:** Aqua blues, bubbles, gloss overlays, rounded UI
- **Vibe:** Futuristic, clean — tech, fintech, Web3

## 42. Dark Magic Academia
Gothic academia × mysticism — Harry Potter × occult.
- **Elements:** Candles, spellbooks, antique etchings, black/gold
- **Vibe:** Mysterious, scholarly — fantasy games, book clubs

## 43. Light Academia
Soft, poetic, classical — European library in summer.
- **Elements:** Creamy neutrals, serif fonts, books, sunlight, linen
- **Vibe:** Scholarly, calm — journals, study apps

## 44. Wabi Sabi
Japanese embrace of imperfection — natural, asymmetrical.
- **Elements:** Earthy tones, rough pottery textures, natural light, organic
- **Vibe:** Humble, calm — wellness, tea packaging

## 45. South West / Wild West
American desert, cowboy culture, Native American influences.
- **Elements:** Terracotta, cacti, denim, cowboy boots, western serif
- **Vibe:** Rugged, adventurous — ranch brands, outdoor gear

## 46. Nautical
Sea life, ships, coastal living — fresh and orderly.
- **Elements:** Navy/white, anchors, ropes, stripes, brass
- **Vibe:** Fresh, maritime — seafood brands, beach resorts

## 47. Rebus
Symbols/images representing sounds or words.
- **Elements:** Pictograms, visual puns, image-type hybrids
- **Vibe:** Witty, playful — educational design, puzzles

## 48. Glassmorphism
Frosted glass — transparent with blur and depth.
- **Elements:** Frosted backgrounds, blur effects, semi-transparency, soft shadows
- **Vibe:** Futuristic, sleek — apps, OS UI, landing pages

## 49. Modular Typography
Type broken into grids/blocks — experimental.
- **Elements:** Geometric letterforms, typographic grids, uniform spacing
- **Vibe:** Structural, modern — posters, branding systems

## 50. Neo-Brutalism
Cleaner evolution of Brutalism — raw but usable.
- **Elements:** Bold colors, large type, stark layouts, asymmetry
- **Vibe:** Confident, bold — portfolios, agency sites

---

## How to Use This With AI

1. **Start prompts with a style name** — "Ethereal UI design" or "Neo-Brutalist web layout"
2. **Mix styles** — "Vaporwave × Gothic" or "Luxury Typography meets Aurora"
3. **Build moodboards faster** — generate 3-4 styles for the same concept
4. **Brief teams** — "Make it feel like Art Nouveau meets Kawaii"

> The problem wasn't the AI. It was the lack of a shared visual vocabulary.
